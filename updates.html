01Departing residence rent — no lease required
FANNIE MAE SEL-2026-08 · PUBLISHED SEPT 2, 2026
The takeaway: a lease agreement is no longer required to count rent on the home you're leaving behind. Market rent — documented with an appraisal, Form 1007, or third-party rent data — can help you qualify for the next home while keeping the old one.
▸ THE FULL DETAILS
1
No lease required

A lease agreement is not permitted for the departing residence scenario — market support replaces it entirely.

2
Market-rent support instead

Document rent with an appraisal showing market rents, Form 1007, or third-party market analysis (Zillow, Redfin, MLS, or similar rent tools) supported by at least 3 comparable rentals.

3
How the income is calculated

Monthly gross market rent × 75%, then subtract the full payment (PITIA). Positive adjusted net rental income may offset that payment only; negative income counts against DTI.

4
The reserve rule

If you have less than 12 months of property-management experience, plan on 6 months of payment reserves for the vacated property.

WHEN THIS APPLIES

  • You're vacating a current primary residence
  • The home is converting to an investment property
  • You're purchasing a new primary residence

WHAT CHANGED

  • Before: lenders often looked to a lease to support rent
  • Now: rent may be supported by market analysis instead
  • Clearer documentation options for departing residences
Source: Fannie Mae Selling Guide Announcement SEL-2026-08 and Selling Guide Chapter B3-3.8 (published September 2, 2026). Summary only — current agency guidance and applicable overlays control.
02Personal property can stay in the contract
CONVENTIONAL GUIDELINE UPDATE · EFFECTIVE AUG 31, 2026
The takeaway: the fridge, the hot tub, the furniture — non-realty items can now remain in the purchase contract without being treated as an inducement to purchase, as long as six conditions are met. No more removing them with an addendum.
▸ THE FULL DETAILS
✓
What counts as a sales concession

Sales concessions are interested-party contributions (IPCs) in the form of non-realty items — cash, furniture, automobiles, decorator allowances, moving costs, giveaways, or excess financing concessions.

THE SIX CONDITIONS — ALL MUST HOLD

  • Clearly identified in the purchase contract
  • Customary for the local market
  • No contributory value assigned

 

  • Contract says items remain for the seller's convenience
  • Appraiser excludes the items from value and valuation analysis
  • Items don't influence price, marketability, or appraised value

PREVIOUS GUIDANCE

  • Remove personal property from the contract with an executed addendum

WHAT CHANGED

  • The items can stay in the contract when every condition above is satisfied
Summary of the conventional IPC guideline update effective August 31, 2026 — current agency guidance and applicable overlays control.
03Bank statement loans — income, seen differently
NON-QM · SELF-EMPLOYED BORROWERS
The takeaway: if your tax returns don't reflect your real business cash flow, 12–24 months of bank statements can establish qualifying income instead — and you keep claiming your valid business deductions.
▸ THE FULL DETAILS
1
How qualification works

12 or 24 months of statements → eligible deposits are averaged → qualifying income after applicable adjustments.

2
Income may qualify higher

Deposit-based income may exceed the net income shown on your returns.

3
Keep valid deductions

Claim allowable business expenses under the usual tax rules — the loan itself creates no deduction, and tax savings vary.

4
More financing paths

Some programs allow both purchases and refinances.

Program terms and expense adjustments vary; compare total loan costs and ask your tax adviser about deductions. Sources: Angel Oak Mortgage Solutions, Bank Statement Loans; IRS Publication 334 (2025).
